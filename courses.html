Risk Management

Study position sizing, stop-loss orders, and the importance of protecting capital.

position sizing is the process of calculating how many units,lots or contracts to buy or sell to control your financial risk on a single trade. rather than guessing or trading a fixed size, it ensures that if your stop loss is hit, you only lose a predetermined and safe percentage of your account capital

Why Learn Risk Management?

Risk management helps traders understand potential losses, use stop-loss orders, and make informed decisions. Forex trading involves significant financial risk, and losses are possible.
